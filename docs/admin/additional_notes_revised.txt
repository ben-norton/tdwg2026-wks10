AI and the People Who Do the Work
Additional workshop notes (WKS10), 24 September 2026
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Position: AI takes on repetitive work. People remain responsible for
change, communication and judgement.

1. The hardest problem is people, not technology.
   The most difficult part of any software project is persuading people to
   change how they work every day. That takes communication skills and
   time. AI cannot do it.

2. AI reduces the labour a task requires.
   The same work takes less time and costs less.

3. Time saved is time to reinvest.
   Staff can use the reclaimed hours to learn new skills and take on work
   that was previously out of reach.

4. Demand is higher than ever.
   The need for software and other technical work keeps growing. AI lets
   people move up to higher-level tasks: design, decisions, review and
   working with colleagues.

5. There are real problems. The biggest is for entry-level workers.
   The routine tasks that AI now absorbs are the tasks people have
   traditionally learned on. We need new ways to train people and bring
   them into the field.

6. We are social creatures.
   People will always look for contact with other people. For now, some
   tasks must be done by humans, above all communication between people.

7. Many AI models work by pattern matching.
   That is why they are good at repetitive, well-defined transformations,
   and why their results must always be checked.
